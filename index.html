About

The tool is rarely the hard part. The hard part is finding the real problem behind a complaint, and then making the fix simple enough that people actually use it.

So far that has meant turning churn scores into actions a team can take, automating multi-step jobs so they recover from failure on their own, and shipping services through a pipeline instead of by hand. I want to bring the same approach to design teams: watch how designers work, find the repetitive steps, and build AI-assisted tools and workflows that remove them.

Outside coursework I led the Synergy Technical Club at SRM — running workshops and mentoring juniors, which taught me to explain technical ideas to people who don't share my background.
